Our Story

We Speak Business, Not Tech.

A focused Australian studio — not a faceless agency. Real people, real results, and no runaround.

Golden & Co. is a focused Australian web studio. We build websites, automations and digital tools for small businesses — the tradies, cafes, clinics and local operators who keep the country running.

We started Golden & Co. because too many good businesses were being let down: slow, dated websites that scared customers off, and hours lost every week to admin that software could handle in seconds.

Our approach is simple — plain English, fixed pricing, and work we're proud to put our name to. No jargon, no middlemen, no faceless account managers. You deal directly with the person building your site, so answers are fast and nothing gets lost in translation.

Whether you need a brand-new website, automated invoicing, or an online booking system, we make the tech side easy — so you can get back to running your business.
