Our Story

A home away from home, grounded in community.

Founded by Sam & Kim, Grounders Café was built on a simple idea — a welcoming space where neighbours, workers and families could slow down, connect and enjoy genuinely good food and coffee.

We take our coffee seriously, pouring a locally roasted blend chosen for our community. Everything is made to be shared, in a space designed for everyone — prams, little ones and all.
